🚀 Just completed my project: **E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics**

I analysed 2 years of simulated Indian e-commerce data (5,000 customers • 28K+ orders • ₹16.6 Cr revenue) to answer one question: *where does the money actually come from — and where is it leaking?*

🔍 What I found:
• Champions = 24% of customers but ~51% of revenue
• Top 20% of customers drive ~63% of sales
• Electronics = 67% of revenue but only ~10% margin, while Fashion earns 28%
• Grocery is slightly loss-making once discounts are applied
• Retention falls from 38% (Month 1) to 16% (Month 6) — the first month is the biggest leak

🛠️ What I built:
✅ KPI & sales trend analysis
✅ RFM customer segmentation (8 segments)
✅ Cohort retention heatmap
✅ Customer Lifetime Value by acquisition channel
✅ K-Means clustering
✅ 10 business SQL queries (CTEs, window functions)

💡 Recommendations: win-back campaign for high-value lapsed customers, a 30-day second-purchase offer, rework Grocery discounts, and shift budget toward higher-CLV channels.

Tech: Python • Pandas • Scikit-learn • Seaborn • SQL
